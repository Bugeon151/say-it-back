# Pilot protocol (two short talks)

Pairs: one person who speaks only English (in the grandchild role) and one older adult who speaks only Korean (in the grandparent role). Report the pair honestly, for example "an English-speaking teenager and a Korean-speaking older adult, not related".

## Before
- Explain what will be recorded and that recordings stay on the laptop. Get a yes from both people (and a parent or guardian for anyone under 18). Ask separately whether screen and voice may appear in the public demo video.
- Korean for the older adult: "대화를 녹음하고 번역해 보는 시험이에요. 녹음은 이 노트북에만 저장돼요. 영상에 목소리가 나와도 괜찮으세요?"
- Desktop Chrome 138+, a microphone, and the language pack downloaded in Setup.

## Talk 1 (about 10 minutes)
1. Three questions from the list, at least one follow-up.
2. At least one "Say it yourself" line.
3. Finish, keep two short lines, have the older adult record them, practise.

## Talk 2 (a day later if possible, about 10 minutes)
1. Turn on "Hide the English until I tap".
2. Three questions and the kept lines.
3. Finish and practise.

## After: System Usability Scale (each person, 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)
1. I think that I would like to use this system frequently.
2. I found the system unnecessarily complex.
3. I thought the system was easy to use.
4. I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this system.
5. I found the various functions in this system were well integrated.
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system.
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use this system very quickly.
8. I found the system very cumbersome to use.
9. I felt very confident using the system.
10. I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system.

Score: for odd items subtract 1 from the answer; for even items subtract the answer from 5; add the ten results and multiply by 2.5 (0–100). An average score is about 68.

Two open questions: "What was the best moment?" and "What got in the way?"
